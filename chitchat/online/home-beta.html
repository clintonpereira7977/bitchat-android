A little hello, in five steps.

  1. Download the APK on each Android phone. Open it from Files / Downloads and allow installation when Android asks.
  2. Choose Online. Enter a nickname and the private pilot invitation supplied by the person inviting you.
  3. Share your public eight-character ChitChat ID. Your friend enters it under Add a friend.
  4. Accept the connection request, then open the contact and say hello.
  5. Enable optional notifications in Online mode. Android may ask for notification permission.

If an older Bluetooth-only test APK causes a signature conflict, uninstall that old build first. Once you have an Online ID, uninstalling or clearing app data loses it. This pilot has no account recovery.

A fresh page.

Opened messages and drafts clear from this phone when you leave or background the app. Unopened messages can wait encrypted for up to 24 hours. Each person controls their own copy.

Messages are encrypted on the phones. This pilot has not had an independent security audit. Notifications require Google Play services and depend on the phone and network.
