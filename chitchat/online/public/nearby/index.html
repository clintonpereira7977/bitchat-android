This iPhone web client joins rooms opened in ChitChat on Android. Open it in Bluefy on iPhone. Safari and Chrome on iPhone don’t provide Bluetooth access for this page.

Your friend opens a room; you send a request; they tap Accept. Keep both apps visible and start 1–2 metres apart.

Messages use the same encryption as ChitChat on Android and travel over Bluetooth. Approval gives permission to chat; nicknames don’t verify identity.

No account or chat server. The page saves only your nickname and app files for offline use when supported. Messages remain in memory and clear on leaving, disconnect or switching away. Your browser, keyboard and phone are outside that promise.

Download the page once online. An “Offline copy ready” label confirms cached app files; verify reopening in airplane mode with Bluetooth on using your own iPhone. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted.

Phone-to-phone compatibility still requires testing on your devices. Bluefy supports joining here; use the native iPhone app if you want either phone to host.
