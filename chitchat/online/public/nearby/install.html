Two ways to join your friend. Keep your existing Android ChitChat app.

01 / FREE · PHONE ONLY

Try it in Bluefy.

The quickest first chat. No computer, developer account or weekly signing renewal.

Get Bluefy ↗
  1. Install Bluefy — Web BLE Browser from the App Store and allow Bluetooth.
  2. Copy this page’s address and open it inside Bluefy. Bookmark the ChitChat home page there for next time.
  3. Your friend opens a room in ChitChat on Android. You tap Find a friend, choose their device, and they accept.
Open ChitChat ↗

Messages travel over Bluetooth. Load the page online first. The home page shows whether an offline copy is ready; test reopening with internet off on your own iPhone. Keep both apps visible. This web version joins Android-hosted rooms.

02 / FREE · WINDOWS SETUP

A place on your Home Screen.

The standalone native iPhone app. Either phone can host a room. Requires iOS 16 or later.

Download iPhone app (.ipa) ↓

The IPA needs signing with your own Apple Account during installation. It won’t install by tapping it in Files.

  1. On Windows, follow the official SideStore prerequisites to install Apple drivers and iloader.
  2. Connect and trust your iPhone by USB. Follow SideStore’s installation guide to install SideStore and complete developer trust, pairing and Developer Mode.
  3. With Wi-Fi and SideStore’s LocalDevVPN enabled, open SideStore → My Apps → + and select the ChitChat IPA from Files.
  4. Open ChitChat from your Home Screen, allow Bluetooth, and connect with your friend.
  5. Refresh both SideStore and ChitChat before seven days pass. After initial setup, renewal can happen from the iPhone over Wi-Fi.

Prefer a simpler first install with ongoing PC renewal? Sideloadly for Windows can sign and install the same IPA. Its free-account app also needs renewal every seven days.

Full Windows setup guide ↓

Free Apple accounts permit three active sideloaded apps, including SideStore. The signing tools need internet for installation/renewal; ChitChat uses Bluetooth to chat.

Made for a little hello.

No account or chat server. Both alternatives use ChitChat’s existing encrypted protocol and connection approval. Messages clear when you leave, disconnect or switch away. Nicknames are not verified identities.

These builds still need Android–iPhone tests on your devices. Try messages in both directions, decline, and leave/reconnect before relying on them.

For an invitation-link native install without weekly signing, the next distribution step is TestFlight through a paid Apple developer team. A TestFlight build expires after 90 days; an App Store release is the long-term route.
